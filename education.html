EDUCATION

strengthenING educational initiatives, ONE book AT A TIME

 Our education initiative is structured to increase access to educational materials, which would otherwise be inaccessible to low-income citizens, due to cost restrictions.   


 

Empowering Communities: Education

educational materials and resources

 We aim to provide educational materials and resources to support existing literacy directives in our communities. One empowered mind can spearhead innovation that energizes and positively impacts a community, the nation, and the world.  
 

book DRIVES

Our strategic long-term goal is to partner with Title I public schools to organize book fairs that allow children to access books ranging in genre - from the creative arts to mathematical sciences. In addition to donating books to adult literacy centers, we are also working towards providing books to children in foster care.
 

Our Impact

The impact a book can have on the curious mind is indeterminable. We are positioned to provide support for educational initiatives in the local communities we serve.

inaugural lecture series: supplemental resource: MAths With

In keeping with our June 2025 report focusing on literacy — and working to offer tangible solutions to the issues raised in the report  — we are actively developing  lecture series that offer supplementary educational resources geared towards supporting adult literacy initiatives.


Our inaugural lecture series, delivered by our President, Olufunlola Arowolo, Ph.D., has been curated for adult learners seeking supplemental resources in preparation for the Mathematical Reasoning portion of the GED.

Video

This lecture series covers the entire Math section of the Kaplan GED PREP PLUS 2022-2023 textbook . Using the Kaplan exam prep method, this inaugural lecture series presents a comprehensive overview of the mathematical concepts assessed in the Mathematical Reasoning section of the exam.
